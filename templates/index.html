How to use this map
Click any road or intersection to open its live control panel — block it, boost its flow, simulate a peak-hour surge, or tag it as a hospital / school / emergency zone. Blocking or jamming a road highlights the detour traffic will now take.
Find optimal route
The route minimises a blend of distance and live citywide congestion (travel time under the network's current BPR/signal-delay weights) — not just the shortest path.
Assigned special zones
No roads tagged yet. Click a road on the map and use "Assign zone" to mark it as a hospital, school, or emergency zone.
Traffic-camera analysis
Click a road on the map to select it, then upload a short clip. The server runs OpenCV background-subtraction + contour counting to estimate a live congestion score from actual vehicle motion, and applies it to that road for ~10 simulated minutes — the same "camera → congestion" IoT path a real CCTV/YOLOv8 pipeline would use.

Demo vehicle batch
Spawn a small tracked batch of vehicles and step them forward manually — separate from the always-on background simulation above. They still route against the live, congestion-aware graph, so they react to any block/jam/boost/zone change exactly like real traffic.
